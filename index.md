Camera

## A camera that knows you're dancing.

Recording yourself shouldn't mean running back and forth to the phone. Dancify's camera counts you in, keeps you in focus and stays out of the way.

- ** Count-in at your tempo**

A musical 5, 6, 7, 8 with a click and haptics, so you're on your mark when recording starts.

- ** Follow**

Tap a dancer and focus and exposure stay on them as they move. Keep in frame zooms gently to hold them.

- ** Motorised stands**

With a DockKit stand, the stand itself turns to follow you across the room.

- ** Every lens**

 0.5×, 1×, 2×, pinch or the zoom dial. Tap to focus, hold to lock focus and exposure.

- ** Slow motion and 4K**

HD or 4K at 30 or 60 fps, and slow motion at 120 or 240 fps for turns and jumps.

- ** Record from across the room**

Start with the volume buttons or Camera Control; auto-stop ends the take. Mirror, torch, grid and level are there too.

Practise with a reference

## Dance along to the video you're learning from.

Play a reference while you film: your own earlier take, a class video, or anything from Photos or Files. Picture-in-picture or side by side, it starts on the count-in's downbeat, so you're together from the first count.

- ** Picture-in-picture or side by side**

Keep the reference small in a corner, or give it half the screen.

- ** Starts with you on 1**

The reference waits for the count-in and starts on the downbeat.

- ** Out of your take**

Muted while you film so it stays out of your recording, unless headphones are connected.

- ** Remembered per piece**

Each piece keeps its reference and tempo ready for next time.

Player

## Loop the eight-count you keep missing.

Set an A–B loop around the tricky phrase, slow it down, mirror it and count it. The player is made for drilling, not just watching.

- ** A–B loop**

Mark A and B and it repeats, with an optional eight-count pause between reps to reset.

- ** Counts on screen**

 1 to 8 in time with the music. Tap the tempo, then jump forward or back by an eight-count.

- ** Slow motion to 0.25×**

And frame by frame, to check a landing, a line, or where the arm really goes.

- ** Mirror**

Flip the picture to see it the way you'd see it in the studio mirror.

- ** On the big screen**

Full screen, AirPlay or an external display, for the whole class to see.

FAQ

## Questions, answered

Something else? Write to [appriseven@gmail.com](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com).

### Is Dancify free?

Yes. Recording, the calendar, timeline, notes, the player, the editor and iCloud sync are free, with no ads and no account. Dancify Premium adds progress reel export, side-by-side compare and the pose guide; it's yearly with a one-week free trial, or monthly.

### Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, iPhone Duo included. There's no Android or web version.

### Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up at all. Your diary lives on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on.

### Where are my videos stored? Can anyone else see them?

Your videos stay on your device. With iCloud sync on, they're also kept in your private iCloud so your other iPhone or iPad can show them. We can't see them, and nothing is uploaded to a server of ours.

### How does the count-in work?

Set a tempo, tap record, and Dancify counts you in with a click and haptics, “5, 6, 7, 8”, while you walk to your mark, so you're in place when it starts.

### What is Follow?

Tap a dancer in the camera and Dancify keeps focus and exposure on them as they move, holding on if they're briefly hidden. Turn on Keep in frame to zoom gently with them, or use a DockKit motorised stand that turns to follow.

### Can I practise along to a video?

Yes. Pick a reference, your own earlier take or a video from Photos or Files, and it plays picture-in-picture or side by side while you film, starting with the count-in. It's muted in your recording unless headphones are connected.

### Can I slow down and loop one part?

Yes. The player goes down to 0.25× and steps frame by frame. Set an A–B loop around a phrase, with an optional eight-count pause between reps, and turn on counts to see 1 to 8 in time with the music.

### Does editing change my original video?

No. Every edit exports as a new take, so the original stays exactly as you filmed it. Share the result or save it to Photos.

### Which dance styles is it for?

Any style you practise with counts or music: ballet, contemporary, jazz, hip-hop, heels, K-pop covers, salsa and bachata, ballroom, tap, Irish dance, commercial and cheer.

### Does Dancify use AI?

No generative AI, and nothing is sent to a server. Follow and the pose guide use Apple's on-device frameworks, so they run entirely on your iPhone or iPad.

### Which languages is the app in?

English, German, Finnish, Spanish, French and Japanese.
